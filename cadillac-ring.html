The Cadillac Ring

It sits flush, so you can stack your favourite rings together.

After hundreds of engagement rings, one wish kept coming up: a band that sits right beside it. So the Cadillac Ring was designed for the band from the first sketch.

The emerald cut is all clarity: long, clean steps of light. The Cadillac sides carry the line across your hand. Underneath, the shank flows up into the setting.

The details

  • 18ct yellow gold with a polished finish.
  • An emerald-cut diamond, natural or lab, held in four claws.
  • Two Cadillac-cut side diamonds.
  • Designed and made in Sydney. Made to order.
  • Presented in a Mets box with a Mets certificate and a note from Aaron.

Looking after the Cadillac Ring
